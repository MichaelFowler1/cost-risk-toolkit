## Government Work

**Government Work** means work that you, or the organization you work for, do
for or under a Government Agreement, including:

1. performing it: the analysis, estimates, schedules, assessments and reports
   the agreement calls for;
2. managing it: planning, controlling and reporting its cost, schedule and
   earned value performance, including the data delivered to the Government
   Agency about it (such as an IPMDAR or an integrated master schedule);
3. winning it: preparing a proposal, bid, basis of estimate or cost estimate
   for a Government Agreement.

A **Government Agreement** is a contract, subcontract at any tier, task or
delivery order, grant, cooperative agreement or other transaction agreement
with or for a Government Agency, including one where you are a subcontractor,
supplier or support contractor to someone else who holds it.

A **Government Agency** is any department, agency, office or other
instrumentality of the United States federal government, or of a U.S. state,
territory, tribal or local government.

## For clarity

- Charging for your own time and expertise on Government Work, while using the
  software, is not charging for the software.
- The results you produce with the software (estimates, forecasts, reports,
  spreadsheets and slides) are not the software. This permission and the
  PolyForm license put no limits on them.
- The permission reaches every individual doing Government Work: employees,
  consultants and support contractors alike.
- **Deliverables are Government Work.** Code, models, tools or analyses built
  on the software that a Government Agreement calls for, delivered to the
  Government Agency under that agreement, are part of performing it, not a
  product offered to others under item 2 of "What stays commercial". The
  delivery has to pass on LICENSE, this permission and the Required Notice

  Government Agency may then use what it received for its own Government
  Work under them. What item 2 reserves is offering something beyond the
  agreement: selling or licensing it to other customers, listing it in a
  catalog or on a schedule, or continuing to offer it after the agreement
  that called for it ends.
